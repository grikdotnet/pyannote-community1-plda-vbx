# Real-speech diarization fixture provenance

## Recommendation

Use a short crop of the existing `vbx/example/audios/16k/ES2005a.wav` with a time-clipped copy of `vbx/example/rttm/ES2005a.rttm` for end-to-end tests. The first 30 seconds already include four annotated speakers and overlap; a crop avoids running an eight-minute recording in every test. Keep the full VBx files as upstream reference assets.

## Ownership and reuse terms

- `ES2005a` is an [AMI Meeting Corpus recording](https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/corpus/signals.shtml). AMI's [official corpus home](https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/) and [download page](https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/download/) state that its public signals and manual annotations are distributed under **CC BY 4.0**. The official [headset-audio index](https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/AMICorpusMirror/amicorpus/HeadsetAudio/) lists `ES2005a.Mix-Headset.wav`. Credit the AMI Meeting Corpus and retain a link to its license when distributing a derived crop.
- The [VBx README](https://github.com/BUTSpeechFIT/VBx#usage) says AMI recordings are downloaded separately, while its VAD and reference RTTMs come from [BUTSpeechFIT/AMI-diarization-setup](https://github.com/BUTSpeechFIT/AMI-diarization-setup). That setup says its diarization references are derived directly from AMI manual annotations v1.6.2, with words merged into speaker turns. Its Apache 2.0 repository license covers the setup's contribution; the underlying annotated speech retains AMI provenance. The [AMI download page](https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/download/) explicitly identifies manual annotations v1.6.2 as CC BY 4.0.
- The [VBx example runner](https://github.com/BUTSpeechFIT/VBx/blob/master/run_example.sh) pairs `example/audios/16k/ES2005a.wav` with `example/rttm/ES2005a.rttm` for diarization scoring. The checked-out WAV is mono, 16 kHz, 16-bit PCM, about 477.88 seconds and 15,292,120 bytes (SHA-256 `AC33E7B18AE72931E65DC279AF4B129370AD62CA6ED64299B9E35F356E3F8ED8`). Its RTTM has 91 speaker turns, four speaker labels, and a final annotated end at 306.608 seconds. The paired [VBx VAD file](https://github.com/BUTSpeechFIT/VBx/blob/master/example/vad/ES2005a.lab) likewise ends at 306.590 seconds. Thus, the annotation does **not** cover the WAV's entire duration. I did not find a primary-source explanation for that cutoff; avoid scoring the full WAV as if the unannotated tail were confirmed nonspeech. These are local file observations; I did not establish byte-for-byte identity between VBx's WAV and the AMI-hosted mix, so describe it as the VBx example of AMI meeting ES2005a rather than an unmodified official AMI binary.
- The [AMI data-problems page](https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/corpus/dataproblems.shtml) says the beginning of ES2005a was not recorded and available material lasts about eight minutes. This is consistent with the checked-out WAV duration; it does not by itself prove binary identity.

## Fixture construction check

For a 30-second crop starting at zero, clip each RTTM interval to `[0, 30]`, drop empty intersections, preserve overlapping turns, and leave speaker IDs as labels rather than expected output IDs. Confirm audio and RTTM use the same timeline, then score with speaker-label permutation and an explicit collar policy. Record the crop command, source SHA-256, resulting SHA-256, and AMI/BUT attribution alongside the fixture. Do not extend the reference to the WAV's unannotated tail without a separate annotation source.
